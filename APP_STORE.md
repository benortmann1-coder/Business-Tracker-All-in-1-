## 1. Final Name: **Bevelry**

Bevelry is a coined word built on the woodworking term *bevel* (an angled cut joining two surfaces). It is short (7 letters), distinctive, brandable in the lineage of Linear / Notion / Stripe, and far less likely to collide with the existing "Bevel" reading app (Matter) or the "Bevel" grooming brand (P&G) than the bare word alone.

## 5. Pricing Strategy

### Recommended Launch Pricing

| Tier | Price | What's included |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Base App (one-time)** | **$9.99 USD** | All MVP features fully unlocked offline: projects, cut list, board-foot calc, material estimator, quotes, invoices, CRM, tool inventory |
| Cloud Sync (subscription) | $4.99/mo or $39.99/yr | Multi-device sync, encrypted cloud backups |
| CNC File Manager (one-time IAP) | $14.99 | .DXF/.SVG/.CNC upload, preview, and per-project organization |
| Team Collaboration (subscription) | $9.99/mo per seat | Shared project boards, task assignment, role-based access |

### Pricing Rationale
- $9.99 is the **sweet spot** for a paid pro tool on App Store: high enough to signal quality and filter casual users, low enough to be an impulse purchase for working pros billing $50–$150/hr.
- Most "free with subscription" competitors charge $15–$50/mo — our paid-up-front + optional add-ons model is cheaper for solo woodworkers and only scales costs for users who actually need cloud/team features.
- The base app must be **fully usable offline with zero subscription required** — this is the core differentiator and a hard rule.

### Launch Pricing Experiments
- **Pre-order discount:** $6.99 for the first 30 days post-launch to drive reviews and surface in "New Apps We Love" categories.
- **Trade group bundles:** Coordinate with woodworking associations / YouTubers for a coupon code or affiliate link at the standard price.

## 6. Competitive Analysis

| App | Audience | Pricing | Strengths | Gaps Bevelry fills |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Premier Build** | General contractors | Free + subs | Project + estimate workflow, decent UX | Not woodworking-specific; no cut list, no board-foot, no CNC |
| **Houzz Pro** | Designers / GCs | $65–$249/mo | CRM, lead gen, takeoffs | Expensive; designer-focused; weak for shop workflows |
| **JobNimbus** | Roofers, HVAC, GCs | $25+/mo per user | CRM + scheduling | Trade-specific to roofing/HVAC; not woodworking |
| **BuilderPrime** | GCs / remodelers | $99+/mo | Quotes, lead capture | Sales-pipeline-first; not project-execution-first |
| **Sortly** | Inventory mgmt | $24–$74/mo | Strong tool/material inventory | Inventory only — no quoting, CRM, or cut lists |
| **CutList Optimizer / SketchList 3D** | Hobbyist woodworkers | Free / $300 desktop | Cut list math is excellent | Desktop-only; no CRM, quoting, or business features |
| **Spreadsheet / Notion / paper** | Most solo woodworkers today | Free | Flexible, no learning curve | No automation, no PDFs, no calculations, slow |

### Competitive Advantages
1. **Only mobile-first app that combines cut list + board-foot + CRM + quoting in one place.** Competitors do one slice well; nobody covers the full shop workflow.
2. **Built for woodworkers, not GCs.** Vocabulary, units, material types, and workflows match how cabinet makers and furniture builders actually work.
3. **Paid up front, no required subscription.** Honest, tradesperson-friendly pricing. Users own the tool they bought.
4. **Works offline.** Shop signal is bad; competitors are mostly online-only SaaS.
5. **Optimized for dusty hands and shop conditions.** Large hit targets, high-contrast UI, glanceable status pills.
