## Full description (4,000 max)

```
WealthTrack is a private bill tracker and paycheck planner. It shows exactly which bills each paycheck has to cover, what's left over, and what's coming up, without linking your bank or creating an account.

YOUR MONEY STAYS ON YOUR PHONE
• Works offline. Your records are stored only on your device.
• No bank login, no ads, no tracking.
• Optional PIN lock encrypts your data, plus fingerprint unlock.
• Optional automatic backup to your own Google Drive.

PAYCHECK PLANNER
• Add one or more jobs paid weekly, every 2 weeks, twice a month or monthly.
• Every bill is assigned to the paycheck before it's due, and you can move it to another check.
• Set aside money for food and gas from each check.
• See what's left from each paycheck and get warned when the next one will come up short.

BILLS THAT HANDLE REAL LIFE
• Weekly, every 2 weeks, monthly, quarterly, yearly, custom or one-time bills.
• Each month keeps its own paid/unpaid record. Pay part of a bill, or pay next month's early.
• Change a bill for this month only or for every month going forward.
• Move a due date, skip a month, pause or cancel a bill.
• Bills due on the last day of the month land on the right day every month.
• Reminders before bills are due, even when the app is closed.

SEE WHERE YOU STAND
• Dashboard with income, bills, paid and unpaid, what's left after bills and your available balance.
• 60-day cash projection so you can spot a short week before it happens.
• Calendar of bills and paydays. Drag a bill to move it for one month.
• Net worth and progress over time.

EXPENSES, DEBT AND GOALS
• Track spending by category, with receipt photos.
• Credit cards and loans with APR, minimums and utilization. Interest is added for you.
• Savings goals with a projected finish date.
• No double counting: card payments and transfers to savings aren't counted as spending twice.

WEALTHTRACK PRO (one-time purchase)
• Automatic Google Drive backup and restore
• Reports, 12-month trends and Excel / CSV export
• Debt payoff planner: snowball vs. avalanche, with interest saved
• Smart Budget: a personalized plan for your take-home pay

Your data is always yours: free JSON backup and restore are included for everyone.

WealthTrack is for budgeting and tracking. It doesn't move money, offer loans or give financial advice.
```
